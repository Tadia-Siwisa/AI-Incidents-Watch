# Research documentation hub

This directory is the stable home for the **AI Agent Incident Watch research record**, independent of data workbooks and scripts.

## Start here
- **[RESEARCH_LOG.md](RESEARCH_LOG.md)** — append-only, dated record of what was examined, decided, completed, corrected, and left unresolved.
- **[RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](RESEARCH_AGENDA.md)** — primary research question, supporting questions, current research phase, definitions and analysis safeguards.
- **[ARTIFACT_INDEX.md](ARTIFACT_INDEX.md)** — links to the existing reports, analysis outputs, source ledgers and scripts elsewhere in the repository.

## Current position — 9 November 2026

- Source-evidence classification complete at desk-audit level: **188 sources** in five categories (**91 / 48 / 32 / 15 / 2**).
- A primary observation: **139 / 188 sources (73.9%)** arise from security investigations, vulnerability reports or controlled evaluations, not necessarily operational AI-agent incidents.
- **Working research question:** Under what conditions, and through what mechanisms, do AI agents attempt or perform actions beyond assigned tasks or authorized scope, and what happens when they do?
- **Immediate next research phase:** Identify observed agent actions, induce behavior groupings and count them within each source-evidence category. Outcomes and authorization are evaluated separately after the action inventory.

## How to use this area

At the end of any working session, append to the research log:
**date → question → material → actions completed → findings → decisions → limitations → next action**.

Keep uncertain observations clearly marked. Link evidence and artifacts. Do not alter the research corpus merely to edit the log.

Existing `data/`, `scripts/`, `reports/`, and `analysis/` retain their responsibilities; this directory is the navigational and methodological hub, not a copy of the dataset.
