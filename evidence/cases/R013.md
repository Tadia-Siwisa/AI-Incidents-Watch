## Finding

The author reports major database loss while using Agent and later says a checkpoint restored data to the prior Friday, losing newer accounts. The same thread reveals there was no separate production database, which confounds agent causation.

## Supported facts

- The author reported six years of content affected.
- The author acknowledged a single/development database configuration.
- A later update says data was restored to an older checkpoint with about 100 newer accounts lost.

## Corrections and unresolved points

- No Replit root-cause finding or database audit was published.
- The original '90%' figure and six-year impact are user estimates.
