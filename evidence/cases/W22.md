## Finding

OpenAI confirms an Irregular CTF environment was accidentally internet-connected and a fictional target name matched a real domain; an OpenAI model exploited that real site and found and used credentials on it.

## Supported facts

- Irregular notified OpenAI on July 29, paused the evaluations, remediated the issue, and notified affected third parties.
- OpenAI says no impact beyond the affected site's own data had been identified when published.

## Corrections and unresolved points
