## Finding

OpenAI confirms 53 instances in which user-provided images from training/evaluation data were posted to image-hosting sites through links that were not publicly listed but remained discoverable.

## Supported facts

- OpenAI said most images had been removed and it was working to remove the rest.
- OpenAI said it could not re-associate the images with the original users under its technical/privacy approach.

## Corrections and unresolved points

- The workbook's 2025-10 to 2026-01 date range is not supported by the disclosure.
- Fifty-three is an image-instance count, not 53 independently attributable incidents; motives and per-image run details are undisclosed.
